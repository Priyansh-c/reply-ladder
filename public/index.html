The problem

Your outreach probably isn't broken. Your process is.

Almost everyone who says cold outreach didn't work for them made one of the three mistakes below. None of them get fixed by sending more messages, which is why sending more messages feels like shouting into a void.

01 / FOLLOW-UP

One message, then silence.

You send it, nobody replies, you decide they aren't interested and move on. But most replies never come from the first message. They come from the second and third touch.

02 / POSITIONING

The message is about you.

"Hi, I'm a designer and I do X, are you interested?" reads as a pitch inside three words. Your opening line has one job. Introducing yourself is not it.

03 / CONVERSATION

They reply, and you freeze.

You finally get "sure, tell me more" and then you improvise, over explain, and lose them. Getting the reply is the easy half.

You don't need more messages. You need a message that knows what comes next.

Cold SMS · Module 01
Use when

You have one result you can name, in one market, and you are going after the same trade somewhere else.

Message
hey working with a [trade] in [their market]. they've been signing [number] new [jobs] a month from our system. wanted to test it in your market. are you taking on more or full?
Why it works
A named market makes it specific rather than a blast. "Taking on more or full" is a capacity question, not a sales question, so answering costs them nothing and tells you everything.
Cold DM · Module 02
Use when

Instagram or Facebook, where anything that reads like a sales email gets ignored on sight.

Message
hey 👋 if i send you 3 homeowners looking to [buy what you sell] next week... would y'all be able to handle it, or would your crew want to kill me? 😂
Why it works
It is a capacity question disguised as a joke, so it is almost impossible to read as a pitch. Both answers are useful.
The call · Module 05
Use when

Immediately after they book. This is the highest leverage message in the book.

Message
quick context for our call, brother. 1. what's your rough close rate on estimates these days? 2. what would 2-3 more closed jobs a month actually mean revenue-wise? i'll plug that into projections so the call isn't a waste of anyone's time.
Why it works
They do the arithmetic on what you're worth in their own numbers before you ever pitch, and answering at all is a commitment.

Questions

Before you buy.

Will this work for my niche?+
The worked examples use pool builders and home service contractors, because that is the market I run. The structure is built on how people react to hearing from a stranger rather than on industry language, and Module 00 tells you which line to change for your world. It works best if you sell a service to businesses or professionals. It is a weaker fit if you sell a low cost product to consumers, where the numbers rarely justify one to one outreach.
Do I need a following?+
No. That's the whole point of cold outreach: you go to people directly instead of waiting to be found. Module 06 covers the one inbound play in the book, which is trade groups, and that works without a following too.
Do I need paid tools?+
Not to start. Everything runs off your phone, a free email account and a spreadsheet. Module 00 covers the tooling you eventually want at volume, a CRM that sends and tracks, a scraper for list building and a number verifier, but I would recommend not buying any of it until you have sent your first hundred messages by hand.
What if I have no results yet?+
Then you use Sequence B, which is built for exactly that and is a full track rather than a footnote. Instead of a case study you lead with an offer they cannot lose on, pay per appointment or pay per job. The book also tells you plainly not to borrow someone else's results, and why that ends badly.
How fast will I see something?+
You can read it in about an hour and send your first messages the same day. Replies take days, not minutes. The follow-up ladder runs across thirty days by design, and cutting it short is the most common reason people decide it didn't work.
